# Case Study: SIH PS #26128 — Livestock Disease Early Detection
**Maharashtra State Innovation Society · MedTech Track · SIH 2025**

---

## The Problem (in 2 lines)
Livestock farmers and field veterinarians in rural Maharashtra have no unified way to report, detect, or respond to animal disease outbreaks in real time. By the time a disease is officially reported, it's already 7–14 days old — and the herd is dying.

---

## Our Verdict: 🟢 GO

**Because:** The problem is real, the evaluators live it every day, and almost no good solution exists at the last mile. India loses **Rs. 25,000 crore annually** to preventable livestock disease. That number means the urgency is built-in — you never have to convince the room the problem matters.

The competitive pool is also self-limiting. Most teams see "8 features listed" and walk away. That's your opening.

---

## The Single Biggest Insight That Changed How We Approached It

> **The evaluators are your end users. Stop pitching to them. Demo to them.**

Every other team will build slides explaining the problem. You already know they know the problem — they're from the government department that owns it. Build the 2-minute demo of a simulated FMD outbreak in Nashik, walk them through it as if they're the district veterinary officer receiving the alert, and you've already won the room emotionally before the Q&A even starts.

---

## What We'd Tell a Future Team Picking a Similar Problem

**Pick one thing to be world-class at, not eight things to be average at.**

The PS lists 8 features. Build 5 at surface level and go *deep* on one — we'd pick the **weather-disease correlation layer**, because it's novel (no Indian platform does it), visual (judges can see it immediately), and defensible (IMD seasonal data is public). That one feature, done well, will be more memorable than a complete but mediocre full implementation.

Also: assign one person to spend **4 hours reading FAO disease cards** before the hackathon starts. Triage logic is the core of your product — if a veterinary expert is on the jury and you can't defend your outbreak threshold, the whole demo falls apart.
